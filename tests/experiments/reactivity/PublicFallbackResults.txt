The complete-selection reader improves the data-access phase in free icons,
independently of private selection capabilities. The public mutation itself is
still expensive in classic views: arranged restoration is about 0.62-0.75 s and
clearing about 1.34-1.59 s in this final run. Modern List/Details remain close to
Explorer. Free Tiles has no equivalent free folder layout in Explorer and is
substantially worse: public clear is 6.132 s. That case was not changed by the
array-reader integration (which rejects Tile mode); a separate control measures
the existing qualified private clear and public redraw suspension. That control
finds no useful drawing-suspension gain: public median 5717.749 -> 5960.147 ms;
existing private median 4721.365 -> 4686.751 ms. No redraw change is adopted.
